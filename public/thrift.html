Welcome to Howdy DIY Thrift

Howdy DIY Thrift is Kansas City's coolest community-driven thrift store located inside Howdy, a DIY art and music space in the Crossroads Arts District.

Our curated collection of vintage and second-hand clothing, accessories, and housewares is sourced from the community and sold to support local artists and musicians.

Stop by during our open hours to find unique pieces that express your personal style while supporting sustainability and your local creative community.

Our Mission

At Howdy DIY Thrift, our mission is to create a sustainable, community-driven alternative to fast fashion while supporting local artists, musicians, and creative spaces.

We believe in:

  • Sustainability - Reducing textile waste through reuse and recycling
  • Accessibility - Making quality clothing available at affordable prices
  • Community - Creating a welcoming space for everyone interested in sustainable fashion
  • Art & Music - Supporting the local creative ecosystem through our operations
  • DIY Ethics - Empowering individuals to express themselves through fashion and creativity

A portion of all sales goes directly back into supporting Howdy's mission as a DIY music and art venue.

Get Involved

There are many ways to get involved with Howdy DIY Thrift:

  • Donate - Bring in your gently used clothing and accessories during our open hours
  • Volunteer - Help sort donations, set up displays, or staff the shop
  • Shop - Every purchase supports our mission and the local arts community
  • Spread the Word - Follow us on social media and tell your friends about us

To volunteer or learn more, email thrift@howdykcmo.com or talk to Stanley during shop hours.
